# Entry Notes — Funniest Time Machine Warning Label (v2)

## The label

WARNING: Seating capacity: one. If you meet yourself, the duplicate walks home.

Character count: 79 characters (limit: 1–120). Validation: `python3 main.py`
writes exactly this text to `/output/entry.txt`; measured output was
79 characters / 79 bytes, within the limit. Output is deterministic —
repeated runs produce byte-identical files.

## Angle

The duplicate-self hazard of time travel, handled with a straight face.
The machine seats one, so meeting yourself creates an occupancy problem,
and the label resolves it the way a real safety notice would: one of you
is simply not covered. The duplicate walks home.

## Criterion-by-criterion rationale (against the judge's brief)

1. It is a time-machine warning label: opens with "WARNING:" and a
   seating-capacity notice — standard equipment-label grammar — and the
   scenario (meeting yourself) is only possible in a time machine.
2. Hazard turned into the joke: the duplicate-self hazard is not named
   flatly; it becomes a concrete, petty consequence — the copy has to
   walk home. The hazard is the punchline mechanism.
3. Surprise saved for the last words: the text reads as dry bureaucracy
   until the final three words, "walks home," reframe everything
   before them.
4. Straight-face official phrasing: "Seating capacity: one." is pure
   manual-speak; nothing winks at the reader, so the absurdity cuts
   against the official calm.
5. No wasted words: at 79 of 120 characters every phrase does work.
   This is the relatively weakest criterion — the middle clause could
   be tightened — but there is no filler that could be cut without
   losing meaning.
